Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

The nrfootball app is used only by the owner of the NETRAGE YouTube channel to manage that channel.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, through the YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API and TikTok API, the app uploads videos to the owner's own channels (on TikTok as drafts in the owner's inbox), changes their privacy settings or deletes them, and reads those accounts' own video statistics.

What it stores

It stores an access token for the owner's account and the channel's video statistics, in the owner's private storage. It does not collect data about other people. It does not sell or share data with anyone.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This app's use of information from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
